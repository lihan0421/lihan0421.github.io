Hi, welcome to my blog! 👋

I'm Han Li, a third-year Master's student in the LLM for Software Engineering Lab at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I work on coding agents and repository-level software engineering — building agents that can understand, debug, and repair real-world code repositories.

Here I plan to share:

- **Research notes** — ideas and lessons from working on coding agents, evaluation, and agent orchestration.
- **Paper reading** — summaries of papers I find useful, especially around SWE-bench and agent architectures.
- **Engineering** — practical notes on building and shipping LLM-based developer tools.
